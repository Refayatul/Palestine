Palestinian History, Told From Palestine

Palestine is a homeland with cities, villages, farms, refugee camps, holy places, archives, songs, foodways, family histories, and a people who have refused erasure.

From a Palestinian point of view, the core story is settler colonialism and dispossession: British rule, Zionist colonization, the 1948 Nakba, the 1967 occupation, settlement expansion, Gaza's siege, political imprisonment, exile, and the ongoing demand for return and liberation.

Palestinian Resistance

Popular Resistance

Marches, strikes, sit-ins, village defense committees, prisoner solidarity, and the Great March of Return keep Palestinian demands visible.

These actions center freedom of movement, refugee return, land defense, and an end to military occupation.

Boycott, Divestment, Sanctions

BDS is a Palestinian civil society call to pressure institutions that profit from or normalize apartheid and occupation.

It asks people to withdraw money, legitimacy, contracts, and cultural cover from systems that deny Palestinian rights.

Narrative Resistance

Palestinians document raids, demolitions, siege, checkpoints, killings, censorship, and everyday survival in real time.

Sharing Palestinian voices challenges propaganda that dehumanizes the colonized and hides state violence.

What This Site Stands For

Return Is a Right

Palestinian refugees and their descendants have not surrendered the right to return to the homes, towns, and lands from which they were expelled.

Occupation Must End

Military rule, settlements, checkpoints, raids, home demolitions, land theft, and administrative detention are not normal political disputes. They are structures of domination.

Gaza Must Be Free

The siege of Gaza is collective punishment. Palestinians in Gaza deserve open borders, reconstruction, healthcare, education, safety, and political freedom.

Apartheid Must Fall

Any system that grants rights by ethnicity, controls movement, fragments land, and privileges settlers over indigenous Palestinians must be dismantled.

Palestinian Culture & Heritage

Palestinian culture is resistance against erasure. Tatreez, olive harvests, village names, keys, poetry, dabke, cuisine, archives, and family stories carry a map that colonization tried to destroy.

Culture keeps the homeland present in refugee camps, diaspora communities, prisons, besieged neighborhoods, and every place Palestinians rebuild life after displacement.

Traditional Music

Preserve songs of harvest, exile, steadfastness, prisoners, martyrs, and return

The Future of Palestine

Liberation, Return, Accountability

A free Palestine means the right of return, an end to occupation and siege, Palestinian sovereignty from the river to the sea, freedom for prisoners, protection of land and holy places, and accountability for those who carried out or enabled oppression.

Right of Return

Defending refugees' right to go home and restoring Palestinian presence to erased villages and cities

Land Back

Protecting Palestinian land, water, farms, homes, archives, and sacred places from confiscation and destruction

Self-Determination

Supporting Palestinian political, cultural, economic, and educational power without colonial control
