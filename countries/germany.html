Germany and Palestine

A Palestinian-view country note focused on state responsibility, arms policy, repression of solidarity, and the demand to stop enabling occupation and apartheid.

Accountability Lens

Arms and Military Support

Any government supplying weapons, military technology, or diplomatic cover while Palestinians are occupied and besieged is not neutral.

Anti-Palestinian Repression

Palestinian speech, student organizing, and anti-apartheid protest must be protected rather than criminalized or smeared.

Policy Demand

End arms transfers, protect civil liberties, support accountability in international courts, and stop treating Palestinian freedom as negotiable.
