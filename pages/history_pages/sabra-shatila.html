Sabra and Shatila Massacre (1982)

The Sabra and Shatila massacre was the killing of between 460 and 3,500 civilians, mostly Palestinians and Lebanese Shiites, by the Phalange militia under the command of the Israeli Defense Forces (IDF) in the Sabra and Shatila refugee camps in Beirut, Lebanon, from 16 to 18 September 1982.
