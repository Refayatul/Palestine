Second Intifada (2000-2005)

The Second Intifada, also known as the Al-Aqsa Intifada, was a major escalation of the Israeli–Palestinian conflict that began in September 2000 and lasted until 2005. The uprising was triggered by Ariel Sharon's provocative visit to the Al-Aqsa Mosque in Jerusalem. The Second Intifada was more violent than the first, resulting in the deaths of more than 3,000 Palestinians and 1,000 Israelis.
