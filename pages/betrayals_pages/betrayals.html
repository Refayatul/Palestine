Betrayals of Palestine

Palestine has been betrayed by empires, governments, corporations, media systems, and elites that chose power over Palestinian life.

Structures of Complicity

Colonial Partition

British rule and partition proposals treated Palestine as land to be divided without Palestinian consent.

Western Weapons and Vetoes

Arms transfers, military aid, diplomatic shielding, and UN vetoes protect Israel from accountability.

Normalization

States normalize relations while occupation, settlements, siege, and refugee exile continue.

Corporate Profit

Companies profit from surveillance, weapons, bulldozers, prisons, settlement tourism, and stolen resources.

Media Dehumanization

Corporate media often erases context, sanitizes Israeli violence, and treats Palestinian grief as background noise.

Censorship

Palestinian speech, student organizing, and anti-apartheid campaigns are smeared or suppressed to protect power.

How to Respond

Name Complicity

Track the politicians, institutions, companies, and media outlets enabling occupation and apartheid.

Withdraw Support

Use boycott, divestment, sanctions, and public pressure where institutions refuse accountability.

Protect Palestinian Voices

Defend journalists, students, workers, artists, and organizers targeted for speaking about Palestine.
