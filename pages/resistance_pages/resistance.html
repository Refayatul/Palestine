Palestinian Resistance

Resistance is not one tactic. It is sumud: remaining, remembering, organizing, refusing erasure, and fighting for return and liberation.

Forms of Resistance

Popular Organizing

General strikes, neighborhood committees, prisoner support, land defense, protests, and village protection work build collective power.

Sumud on the Land

Planting, harvesting, rebuilding homes, protecting olive groves, and refusing forced displacement keep Palestinians rooted.

BDS

The Palestinian-led boycott, divestment, and sanctions call pressures institutions complicit in occupation, apartheid, and denial of return.

Cultural Resistance

Poetry, tatreez, dabke, posters, oral history, film, and food memory protect identity against colonial erasure.

Documentation

Palestinian journalists, medics, families, and youth record raids, siege, demolitions, and killings when institutions look away.

Right of Return

Keys, village names, refugee organizing, and the Great March of Return insist that exile is not surrender.

Symbols and Memory

Handala

Naji al-Ali's barefoot refugee child watches the world until Palestine is free.

The Keffiyeh

A textile of rural Palestine that became a global symbol of anti-colonial solidarity.

The Watermelon

Its colors carried the flag when Palestinian national symbols were banned.

The Key

Refugee families keep keys as proof of homes, villages, and a right that does not expire.
