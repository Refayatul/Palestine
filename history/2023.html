2023

The year 2023 marked the beginning of what many human rights organizations have characterized as genocide in Gaza, following the October 7 resistance operation known as "Al-Aqsa Flood." Israel's response involved a complete siege of the Gaza Strip and an intensive military campaign that has killed over 30,000 Palestinians, including more than 13,000 children. This period represents both the darkest chapter in recent Palestinian history and the emergence of the most powerful global solidarity movement the cause has ever seen.
